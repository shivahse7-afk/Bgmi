Who Are We?

We are here to bring all students from different disciplines and skill sets to one comprehensive fire safety training platform. From here, they will be in a position to move on a path of knowledge enrichment, skill acquisition, and attitude development that takes them to their professional destination.

Our Objective

EDUCATION - To meet the needs of students aspiring for careers in fire safety.

EDUCATION - To establish Mahatma Gandhi Fire And Safety Institute as a landmark institution for fire safety training across India. This reputation and track record have been built through adherence to excellence and dedication to our formula for success, based on the belief that there is a fire safety professional in every student waiting to be discovered.

We provide an opportunity for students to identify themselves among others and realize their potential compared to the expected standards of the fire safety industry.
